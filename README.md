## 🧬 About Me

> Applied AI engineer and Master of Artificial Intelligence candidate with a combination of peer-reviewed research and production-grade deployments — four publications across Springer, Elsevier, ACM, and arXiv, built alongside real-world systems for a Siemens-backed smart manufacturing initiative and a Tamil Nadu State Government platform.

It started with an undergraduate research internship at **IIT Indore**, where literature reviews and benchmarking turned into three co-authored survey papers and a first-author conference paper published in Springer. That research habit — read deeply, benchmark rigorously, write clearly — carried straight into engineering work: a real-time vehicle detection pipeline deployed for a **Siemens** smart-manufacturing initiative, a hospital management system with a fine-tuned medical chatbot, and a stock-and-memo platform built in direct collaboration with a **State Government**, complete with role-based access control and a live payment gateway.

The common thread across all of it is **computer vision, NLP, and speech modeling** — usually with PyTorch and TensorFlow doing the heavy lifting, and always with an eye toward domains that matter: healthcare, agriculture, public infrastructure, and government service delivery. Now pursuing a **Master of Artificial Intelligence at RMIT University, Melbourne**, while continuing the IIT Indore research remotely, and actively looking for the next AI / data science role in Melbourne.
